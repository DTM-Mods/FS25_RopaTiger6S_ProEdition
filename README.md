ProEdition copies of the base-game **Ropa Tiger 6S** self-propelled beet harvester, its **RR-XL 9x45** header and the header trailer. The models, textures, sounds and settings are the same as the base-game machines, listed in the shop next to the originals:

- **Beet Harvesters:** "Tiger 6S ProEdition"
- **Beet Harvester Headers:** "RR-XL 9x45 ProEdition" and the "ProEdition" header trailer

### What's different

- The header harvests, and the Tiger holds, **every root crop and vegetable** in the base game, plus **sugar cane**: sugar beet, beetroot, potatoes, carrots, parsnips, green beans, peas, spinach, onions and sugar cane (topped and cut, with the game's cane cutting effects and cane on the belts).
- **Tiger shop options:** the ProEdition 57.5 m³ tank, the 996 hp Tiger ProEdition engine, a cab window tint (Light, Medium or Dark), side and rear logos on or off, and three colors: main color theme, mechanical parts (belts and cleaning section) and rims.
- **Header and trailer:** the same main color theme and black color group as the Tiger, so all three machines match; the trailer also has a rim color.

### Multiplayer

Works in multiplayer; every player needs the mod. The unloading and bunker belts stop for every player when unloading ends (fixes a base-game issue where they kept running on servers).
